    THE FELLOWSHIP'S FIELD GUIDE

    A little courage. A little chaos.

    Win: reach level 10 by defeating a monster on your own turn. Helpers and blessing cards can only take you to level 9.

    1. Make camp. Equip a weapon, armor and charm. Replacing an item discards the old one. Spend 3 gold to buy a random card. Play blessing cards here.
    2. Explore. Reveal a random monster. Your strength is level + offense + equipment.
    3. Make a deal. Other players can offer help; accept up to two. Helpers add their full strength and +2 teamwork each. Alliances last one encounter and cannot be revoked after acceptance.
    4. Play your cards. Anyone can boost the fellowship; outsiders can strengthen the monster. Each participant can use their chaos ability once per encounter. Cards and accepted helpers give everyone a 5-second response window; the initial reveal gives 8 seconds.
    5. Fight or flee. Beat the monster's strength strictly; ties lose. Flee succeeds if a d6 + your defense reaches 12, letting the entire party escape. Failed flight costs participants 1 level if their defense meets the monster's danger, otherwise 2; each also loses a random hand card. Levels never go below 1.
    6. Share the spoils. The active player earns the monster's listed levels and cards, plus 2 gold. Every helper earns 1 level (up to 9), 1 card and 1 gold. Discard down to 8 before ending your turn.

    Special alliances: Amelia + Kaitlyn + Samuel add +5 strength; Sean + Matt add +4, on top of normal teamwork.

    Cards: draws are random with replacement, so duplicates are possible. Hands have a storage cap of 20 cards; extra rewards above 20 are lost. Gear stays equipped after defeats. You may discard hand cards at any time during play.

    Reconnecting: keep this tab to preserve your seat. An offline player's turn can be skipped by the host after 90 seconds. Anyone can claim host after the host is offline for 2 minutes. The game does not advance turns automatically.

    An unofficial fan game with original house rules. Your friends' character stats and chaos abilities are editable in cards.h.
